Platform Vision

Enable McKinsey's Modern Research practices with a cloud-native, AI-augmented data platform that delivers trusted insights faster — accelerating the firm's ability to serve clients with data-driven intelligence at scale.

The MRx platform is the single pane of glass for all research data engineering: from raw ingestion to curated, governed datasets that fuel analytics, AI models, and client-facing products.

🚀
Mission Statement

Deliver a production-grade, enterprise-ready data engineering foundation that:

  • Accelerates time-to-insight through config-driven pipelines and AI-assisted development
  • Enforces security, compliance, and governance by design — not by afterthought
  • Reduces operational toil through automated CICD, observability, and self-healing patterns
  • Enables cost-aware, sustainable compute consumption aligned with FinOps principles
  • Empowers engineering teams with standardized frameworks, tools, and best practices

📋 Platform Summary
End-to-end overview of the MRx data engineering ecosystem
What is MRx? MRx (Modern Research Platform) is McKinsey's enterprise Databricks-on-Azure data engineering platform. It provides standardised frameworks for data ingestion, transformation, governance, and AI-powered analytics — all deployed through Terraform-managed infrastructure and managed by GitOps CICD pipelines.
